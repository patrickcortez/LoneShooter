Name: Level system

Player gains xp when killing enemies.

Xp gains per enemy:

Melee grunts: 5xp
Elite Melee: 15xp
Ranged: 10xp
Spearguy Elite: 15xp
Marshall: 200xp
Gravitals: 20xp
Overall Boss: 1000xp

---

Player sees an xp bar right above their hp bar.
sprite of xp bar is in: "assets\player_sprite\player-xp", with names xp-0, xp-10, xp-20 to xp-100,

---

100 xp is needed to level up,

+1 level means +20 xp needed to level up for the next level and so on.

---

every time the player levels up, the game pauses and, they get a ingame window to choose upgrades:

they can:

increase an attribute by 1:

- speed
- health
- damage

which means their mouse is visible until they have clicked what they want to upgrade.
then the window disappears and the game unpauses.

---

players can see their level in the bottom middle part of their screen.
