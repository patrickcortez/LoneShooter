Name: Spear Guy
Type: Elite Melee

Hp: 3 bullet hits
Damage: 10 hp per hit
Speed: fast

Sprite: assets/Spearguy/spearguy-move.bmp

Behavior:

- Always blends in with mininum of 3 melee units, always staying behind them.
- if no melee units more than 3 are around, enters idle phase (sprite: spearguy-idle.bmp), until it detects the appropriate amount of melee units.
- if surrounding melee units has been dealt with and is near player, Spearguy dashes towards the player.
- if dealth with and not near the player, runs to the nearest horde, if no horde nearby retreats and goes into idle phase until horde is near.

 Abilities:

Dash: dashes rapidly 5-7 blocks left or right when being shot at.
- Cooldown: 1s
- changes sprite to assets/Spearguy/spearguy-dashing.bmp

Block: blocks incoming player bullets when dashing is impossible
- Cooldown: 0.5s after blocking player bullet
- changes sprite to assets/Spearguy/spearguy-block.bmp
